Privacy policy for Pixel Tower Defence

Last updated 20 November 2026

Pixel Tower Defence is a free tower defence game for families. It does not collect, send or share any information about you or your children.

What the game does not do

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking and no chat. The game does not ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

What stays on your device

The game remembers player names, settings, levels you make in the Creator and family versions you save. These are kept only on your own device, in the game's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data, removes them.

If your phone's own backup is switched on, Android may copy this storage to your Google account as part of its normal backup, so the saves come back if you reinstall. That backup is run by Google under your Google account, not by this game.

Sharing levels and versions

When you choose to share a Creator level or a family version, the game makes a code or a link that holds only that level or version (for example its name, towers, enemies and map). You decide whether to send it and to whom. The link works without any server: the level travels inside the link itself.

Internet

The game plays fully offline. The only time it uses the internet is if you tap a button that opens a web page, such as the feature request form, which opens in your web browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is made to be safe for children. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be asked on the game's project page: github.com/jpashami/tower-defense-game.
